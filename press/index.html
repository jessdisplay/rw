The marks

Three marks, three colourways. Vector first, PNG where vector is refused.

The arc mark. A sunrise, and the figure standing under it.

Use the SVG wherever the format is accepted; it is the same file the site itself draws from and it holds up at any size. The PNGs are for the places that refuse vectors, and they arrive on a transparent ground rather than a white square, so nothing has to be knocked out by hand.

Rise is the compliance platform for NDIS providers and the auditors who certify them. The whole law is mapped once, so providers build their evidence against the clause and auditors assess it against the same clause.

For providers, Rise reads the law in plain English, keeps policies, procedures, forms and registers current as the rules change, and files evidence against the clause it answers rather than into a shared drive. For auditors, applicants arrive organised against the clauses, which is what takes a certification from forty hours to ten.
