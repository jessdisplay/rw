FAQ's

What is the frequency of the event occurring in a month?

The frequency of the event occurring in a month can vary depending on the specific event. For example, if it's a weekly event, it would occur about four times a month. If it's a monthly event, it would occur once a month. Please provide more details about the event for a precise answer!

What problem does Rise solve?

The Australian government now requires 245,000 NDIS providers to be registered to operate. there are only 20 auditing bodies, and each audit takes 30 hours creating an 840-year backlog to a compliant sector. Large compliance platforms exist but niece markets are completely underserved and looking for solutions.

How Does Rise Solve the Problem?

Under the NDIS, 245,000 providers must register through just 20 auditors, each taking 30 hours per audit an 840-year backlog. Rise automates compliance through a shared digital workspace that connects providers and auditors, cutting audit time from 30 to 10 hours and saving providers up to $280K a year, while improving outcomes for people in care.

What is the GTM and opportunity?

We are partnering with the top 3 auditing bodies controlling 80% of the market, giving Rise direct access to $632M ARR. These auditors operate in 191 countries, creating a clear path to scale across niche global compliance sectors.
